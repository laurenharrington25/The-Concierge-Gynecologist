Expertise

Personalized gynecology care for every stage of life.

Skip to the list of areas

Hormonal health

Perimenopause

Evidence-based tools to manage the hormonal disruptions, mood changes, sleep alterations, cycle variations and sexual function changes that mark the transition toward menopause.

Menopause

Personalized hormonal and non-hormonal therapies to treat symptoms and help you feel like yourself through menopause and beyond.

Sexual function

Expert, sensitive and effective care for changes in desire, comfort, orgasm and sexual function at any life stage.

Cancer survivorship & risk

Cancer survivorship

Specialized care addressing the gynecologic and hormonal effects of cancer treatment, helping women thrive after treatment.

Previvor surveillance

Ongoing gynecologic surveillance and risk-reducing guidance for women with a hereditary predisposition to cancer.

Gynecologic conditions

Abnormal uterine bleeding

Thorough evaluation of irregular, heavy, or unexpected bleeding to identify the cause, mitigate the symptoms, and obtain the best treatment.

Pelvic pain

Thorough evaluation and multimodal treatment of acute and chronic pelvic pain.

Vulvar conditions

Thoughtful evaluation and treatment of vulvar skin conditions, irritation, and pain.

Contraception

Personalized counseling for contraceptives, including Nexplanon and IUD insertion and removal with advanced pain control options.

Procedures

Endometrial biopsy

A minimally invasive procedure to evaluate and treat conditions inside the uterus, such as polyps or abnormal bleeding.

IUD insertion

IUD insertion and removal with advanced pain control options.

Colposcopy & LEEP

For evaluation and treatment of abnormal cervical cells.

Hysteroscopy

A minimally invasive procedure to evaluate and treat conditions inside the uterus, such as polyps or abnormal bleeding.
