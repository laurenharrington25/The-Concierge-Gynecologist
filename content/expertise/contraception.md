## Contraception that fits your life

The best method of contraception is the one that fits your health, your goals and your stage of life. Those needs change over time. A method that worked well in your twenties may not be the right one in your forties, when perimenopause, heavier periods or other health considerations may come into play.

Dr. Harrington takes the time to talk through your options in detail, so you can make an informed choice and feel confident in it.

## Options Dr. Harrington provides

- **Hormonal IUDs,** which last several years and often make periods lighter
- **The copper IUD,** a long-lasting, hormone-free option
- **Nexplanon,** a small implant placed in the upper arm that lasts for several years
- **Pills, patches and rings,** including options that can also help with heavy or painful periods
- **Progestin-only methods,** for those who should avoid estrogen

## Personalized counseling

Choosing a method means weighing effectiveness, side effects, how each option affects bleeding and how it fits with your other health conditions and medications. For women in perimenopause, contraception can sometimes be combined with treatment for symptoms such as irregular or heavy bleeding. Counseling also covers when contraception can safely be stopped as menopause approaches.

## Comfort during insertion

Many people worry about pain during IUD or Nexplanon insertion. Dr. Harrington prioritizes comfort and offers advanced pain control options, which are discussed with you in advance so you know what to expect. See [IUD insertion](/expertise/iud-insertion/) for more about the visit.

## Common questions

### Do I still need contraception in perimenopause?

Yes. Pregnancy is still possible until menopause is confirmed, even if periods are irregular. Dr. Harrington can help you choose a method that also addresses perimenopausal symptoms, and advise you on when it is safe to stop.

### Can an IUD or Nexplanon be removed early?

Yes. Both can be removed at any time if you want to change methods or try to become pregnant.
