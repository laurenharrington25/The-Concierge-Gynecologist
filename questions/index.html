The Practice

Do you take insurance?

Dr. Harrington does not accept insurance for appointments. This allows her to spend as much time needed to get to know you and create a comprehensive plan to address your concerns and symptoms. Your insurance can still be used for services outside of visits with Dr. Harrington: laboratory tests, imaging, and prescriptions (depending on your individual plan).

Where is your office?

Dr. Harrington currently partners directly with concierge primary care practices to see patients on-site at their practices. This allows for coordinated and collaborative wrap-around care for patients, and seamless communication between providers.

How do I become a patient?

Subscribe to the newsletter to be the first to know about new partnerships with practices and opportunities to establish care with Dr. Harrington. If you’re interested in seeing Dr. Harrington, and your concierge primary care physician is not a current partner, please submit an inquiry to Dr. Harrington’s team.

Care and Treatment

What is your philosophy on hormone therapy medications?

Dr. Harrington prescribes FDA-approved medications and treatments whenever one exists for your condition. Together, you and Dr. Harrington may consider compounded or non-FDA-approved options when no approved alternative is available.

Do you only treat perimenopause and menopause, or do you see patients for general gynecologic care too?

While hormone therapy, perimenopause, menopause, sexual function and cancer survivorship are a particular focus, Dr. Harrington also treats the full spectrum of gynecologic conditions, including: contraception, abnormal and postmenopausal bleeding, vulvar conditions, pelvic pain, and abnormal pap smears.

Do you do procedures?

Yes, Dr. Harrington performs procedures, including: hysteroscopy / D&C, endometrial biopsy, vulvar biopsy, colposcopy, LEEP, IUD insertion and Nexplanon insertion. Dr. Harrington prioritizes her patients’ comfort and offers appropriate pain medication.

Coordinated Care

How is this different from online hormone therapy platforms?

Online platforms can be a reasonable starting point, but they typically don’t include an in-person exam, indicated procedures, or ongoing access to the same physician. Dr. Harrington’s model offers comprehensive in-person and telehealth visits with exclusive access and continuity.

Do you offer telehealth visits?

Yes, Dr. Harrington is happy to see you in person or on telehealth.
